The primary analysis classifies both corpora from the class name alone, because
AndroidControl carries no superclass chain. Variant D reclassifies Rico with the
richer signal while leaving AndroidControl untouched, which is the specification
most favourable to Rico that the data permits. The cross-corpus gap under
variant D is therefore the answer to the objection that the 2017 figure is low
because the classifier saw less of it.

Variant E is deliberately hostile to this paper's own headline. If a large share
of the 2024 corpus's descriptions were placeholders, the improvement since 2017
would be partly cosmetic. The variant E row of the coverage table is what
settles that, and it should be reported whether or not it is flattering.

NOTES, ASSUMPTIONS AND LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Limitations
  1. The uninformative-description list is a conservative keyword rule, not a
     semantic judgement. It will miss a description that is fluent but wrong
     ('Search' on a settings button), and that class of error cannot be detected
     from the view hierarchy at all. Variant E is therefore a lower bound on
     low-quality labelling.
  2. None of these variants can distinguish a decorative image from a meaningful
     one, because the view hierarchy does not encode that. The estimates are
     therefore upper bounds on genuine debt, which is the conservative direction
     for the paper's claim.
