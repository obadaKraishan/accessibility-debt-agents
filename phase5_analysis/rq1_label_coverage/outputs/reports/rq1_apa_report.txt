1. SAMPLE AND INCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

An element enters the analysis population when it is flagged visible to the user
and has positive area. Both conditions are necessary: a large share of nodes in
these hierarchies have zero or negative area while still carrying a visibility
flag, so filtering on visibility alone would inflate the denominator and deflate
every rate reported here.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

than a judgement call. Every claim in this paper should survive being restated
