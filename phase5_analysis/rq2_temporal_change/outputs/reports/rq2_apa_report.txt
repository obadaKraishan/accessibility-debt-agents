1. DESIGN AND WHAT THE COMPARISON CAN SUPPORT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Rico and AndroidControl are two independently collected corpora, not two waves
of a panel. Any difference between them confounds genuine change in developer
practice with app selection, platform evolution, and differences in how each
corpus captured the interface. The difference is therefore reported as a corpus
difference throughout. It is not a causal estimate of change over time, and it
should not be written up as one.

NOTES, ASSUMPTIONS AND LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Notes
  1. H2.2 comprises 5 role comparisons; each line reports the unadjusted p with
     the Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted value appended.
  2. A defensible way to state the finding is: the corpus that represents
     contemporary, actively maintained apps shows markedly higher coverage, and
     the residual debt in it is the quantity that matters for agents today.

Limitations
  1. Corpus differences are not causal estimates of change over time. App
     selection, platform evolution and capture method all differ between the two
     datasets and cannot be separated with these data alone.
  2. AndroidControl covers 833 apps chosen to support task demonstrations, which
     is a narrower and probably better-maintained population than Rico's broad
     2017 crawl of 9,384 apps. That alone would push the newer corpus's coverage
     upward.
