1. DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Two coders applied the definitions in CODEBOOK.md to the same 300 stratified
elements, independently and blind both to each other and to the automated codes.
Human-to-human agreement is the inter-rater reliability of the coding scheme.
Each coder is then scored against the deterministic rules in taxonomy.py;
because those rules cannot disagree with themselves, that second comparison
measures the validity of the automated scheme against human judgement, not
reliability. The two are reported separately and should be described separately
in the Method section.

NOTES, ASSUMPTIONS AND LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Notes
  1. Every disagreement is listed with its context in
     codebook_irr_disagreements.csv, with blank columns for the agreed code.
     Adjudicate them together, record the resolution there, and state in the
     Method section how many items were resolved by discussion.
  2. Both coded variables met the pre-set inter-rater reliability target of
     0.80.

Limitations
  1. Kappa is sensitive to the marginal distribution of categories. The sample
     is stratified by role and by automated label status precisely so that rare
     categories are represented; on an unstratified draw the same rules would
     yield a higher kappa with less information in it.
